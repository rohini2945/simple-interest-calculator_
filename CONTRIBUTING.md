# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive. Do not harass, insult, or discriminate.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope
This applies in all community spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement
Report abusive behavior to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Leaders will follow these steps: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
